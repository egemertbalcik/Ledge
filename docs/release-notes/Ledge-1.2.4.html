Ledge stays quick while a browser plays video, and updates now arrive on their own.

  • Video in the browser. Watching a video on X or other sites no longer makes Ledge lag, and the volume keys show Ledge's readout instead of the system pop-up.
  • Updates check themselves. Ledge now looks for new versions in the background, as it was meant to.
  • Live streams. A live stream in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc no longer stops now playing for the rest of the session. The card shows it as live.
  • Play works every time. Pressing Play on a paused card no longer sometimes does nothing.
  • Your card stays open. Pressing a volume or brightness key while the pointer rests on a card shows the readout, then brings the same card back.
  • Settings rests when closed. Closing Settings stops its background checks, and it reopens on the page you left.
