Pasted text comes into Notes clean, and Keep Awake says its piece once.

  • Pasting is clean. Text from a web page, Word or Slack comes into a note without its highlight, colours, fonts or links. Bold and italic come along as the note's own, so the B button lights up and they are still there after a restart.
  • Old notes heal. A note that already picked up a highlight or a colour from a paste loses it the next time you open it.
  • Paste as plain text. ⌥⇧⌘V pastes only the words, without bold or italic.
  • The caret stays where you click. The first key you press after opening a note no longer sends the caret to the end.
  • Keep Awake speaks once. The notch shows it when it starts, then gives the space back to your music. Its card still shows it is on.
