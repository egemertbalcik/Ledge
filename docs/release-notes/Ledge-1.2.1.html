Keep Awake that actually keeps the Mac awake, and media controls that hold on to the song.

  • Coffee keeps the screen on too. It now holds what caffeinate -dmi holds: the Mac, the screen and the disk. Before, the screen went dark on its own and the Mac looked asleep.
  • No more stopping after an hour. A session with no end time ran out after sixty minutes. It now lasts until you switch it off.
  • The Keep Awake card stays put. When a session ends it shows what happened, then goes back to the switch instead of disappearing.
  • Keep Awake says it once and steps aside. Starting it shows the cup briefly, even during Focus, and the notch goes back to rest. With music playing, a small cup sits beside it.
  • Told when macOS holds back. If macOS keeps the Mac awake but refuses the screen, the card says so and Ledge keeps asking.
  • Pausing from AirPods keeps the card. The song stays in the notch, scrubbing stays on the track you meant, and the waveform no longer jumps when you press Play.
