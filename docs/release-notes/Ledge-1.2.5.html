Scroll to set Keep Awake and the timer. Today's calendar shows only what is still to come.

  • Scroll the Espresso length. The days, hours and minutes on the Espresso stop each scroll on their own with a two-finger stroke or the mouse wheel. Swiping sideways still moves between cards.
  • Scroll the timer's rule. With the rule open, a sideways stroke on it moves it the way a drag does. The mouse wheel turns it one minute at a time.
  • Today, what's left. The calendar lists only today's events that haven't ended. An event in progress stays until it ends. Past days still show everything.
  • Camera mirror. The mirror beside Join is the right way round from the first press. It also gives a slow camera more time to start.
