Pick the calendars you see, scrub your music again, and a notch that moves instead of jumping.

  • Scrubbing works again. Dragging the bar in Spotify or Music did nothing in 1.2.1. It now lands where you let go, on the song you were looking at.
  • Choose your calendars. The Calendar card's page in Settings lists every calendar by account. Switch off the ones you don't want in the notch; new calendars show until you switch them off.
  • No surprise Automation dialog. Ledge asks for access to Music and Spotify only when you press Automation in Settings, Permissions. If you already allowed it, nothing changes.
  • Someone else's AirPods stay out of it. Another pair of the same model nearby no longer sets off your low-battery alert again and again, and putting your earbuds in their case doesn't announce a low battery.
  • Notes keep their titles. A renamed note keeps its new title on the card after a restart, and ⌘Z only undoes in the note you're writing.
  • Focus and the timer tell the truth. The Focus card goes away when Focus ends. A pomodoro left running with the lid shut waits for you instead of running on all night.
  • Weather names your town. Not the street or the café you're sitting in. With no place name it says Current Location, not the city you typed.
  • Smoother all round. Cards open in one movement, album art cross-fades between songs, the Keep Awake switch slides between stops, and Settings is laid out like System Settings.
